- Separate classification at b=-1, including c=-5/4, and infinitude of distinct c at every rational b. This is not a statement for every c. Automorphisms require b!=0.

- Normalized rotation quotients with necessary and sufficient arithmetic lifting conditions; marked points are not counted as distinct unmarked orbits.
- Correct real saddle scope for the original explicit section: b outside {-1,0,1}; no nonlinear-stability conclusion from neutral multipliers.
- Explicit limits on the selected algebraic and Lean certificates; the entire article is not formalized.
